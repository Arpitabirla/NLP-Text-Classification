🔵 Porter Stemmer

A simple, fast rule-based algorithm. Removes suffixes using a series of heuristic rules. Fast but can produce non-words (e.g., "beauti" from "beautiful").

🟢 Snowball Stemmer

An improved version of Porter. More aggressive and supports multiple languages. Still rule-based — may create unrecognizable stems.

🟣 Lemmatization

Uses a vocabulary and morphological analysis to return the base dictionary form. Context-aware and always produces valid words (e.g., "running" → "run").

Step 5

TF-IDF Analysis

Term Frequency × Inverse Document Frequency — weighing words by importance.

TF (Term Frequency)
TF(t,d) = count(t in d) / |d|
How often a word appears in this document
×
IDF (Inverse Doc Frequency)
IDF(t) = log(N / (1 + df(t))) + 1
How rare the word is across all documents
=
TF-IDF Score
TF-IDF = TF × IDF
High score = important & rare word

🎓 Key Takeaways

🔤

Tokenization

First step in any NLP pipeline. Splits text into words/sentences for further processing.

✂️

Stemming

Fast but crude. Good for search engines where speed > precision.

📖

Lemmatization

Slower but accurate. Best for tasks needing linguistic precision (e.g., QA systems).

🧮

BOW

Simple and fast. Good baseline for classification. Ignores word order.

⚖️

TF-IDF

More informative than BOW. Penalizes common stop-words naturally.
